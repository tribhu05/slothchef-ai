### 🧠 Dual-Engine AI Architecture

SlothChef decouples culinary prediction into two specialized open models:
1. **Prior Labs TabPFN-3.5 (Tabular Foundation Model)**: Evaluates continuous multi-attribute chemical flavor vectors (Umami, Acidity, Lipids, Saltiness) in a single in-context Bayesian forward pass (<45ms). Calculates mathematical flavor harmony before any text generation occurs.
2. **Google Gemma 2 (Local Open-Weight SLM)**: Operates as the culinary heuristic engine, translating numerical chemical bounds into sequential, zero-friction 1-pan instructions that celebrate radical laziness.

### 🔒 Why Open Innovation Matters
- **Private Fridge Telemetry**: What and when you eat reveals intimate financial and health patterns. Running open-weight models locally ensures your midnight pantry habits stay strictly between you and your machine.
- **100% Offline Resilience**: Operational off-grid in campervans, during power outages, or with zero internet access.
- **Zero Token Anxiety**: No monthly subscriptions, rate limits, or surprise cloud billing meters.
